Terms & policy

Plain rules so everyone knows what to expect. Last updated November 2026.

Orders and payment

  • All orders start in our Discord server.
  • Prices are agreed in the server before work starts.
  • Work begins after the agreed payment or advance.

Delivery and revisions

  • First drafts are usually delivered in 24 to 48 hours.
  • Every order includes up to 3 free revisions on the same idea.
  • Major changes after approval may cost extra.

Usage rights

  • You get full usage rights to the final thumbnail for your own channel.
  • We may show finished work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.
  • You may not resell the design as a template.

Refunds

  • If work has not started, you can get a full refund.
  • Once a draft is delivered, payments are non-refundable.

Privacy

  • We only keep what you share to complete your order.
  • We never sell or share your information.
  • Ask us in the server to delete your files at any time.

Server rules

  • Be respectful to everyone in the community.
  • No spam, scams or self-promotion without permission.
